## Agent steps

1. **Grow a platform while you write.** Log Substack subscribers and open rate monthly. Talks, committee work, ONAS and PFUS all count.
2. **Finish the proposal before you query.** Proposal to readers May 9. When an agent asks, it goes out within 48 hours.
3. **Build a list of 40 agents.** Sunday mornings Apr 18 to May 30. Publishers Marketplace, QueryTracker, Manuscript Wish List, comps' acknowledgments.
4. **Write a 1-page query.** Hook sentence, 1 paragraph on the book, 2 comps, bio + platform, why this agent.
5. **Send 10 at a time.** Jun 1, Jul 13, Aug 10, Sep 7. Personalize each one.
6. **Read the results before each batch.** 0 requests = fix the query. Requests then passes = fix the proposal or samples.
7. **When an offer comes.** Book a call. Ask about recent deals and editing style. Tell every agent still reading, give them 1 to 2 weeks.
8. **After you sign.** Revise the proposal together, agent pitches editors, write the full book on contract.

## Voice check

- Read it out loud
- No em dashes
- No "it's not X, it's Y" or any negate-then-correct line
- No banned words (delve, crucial, leverage, robust, foster, ultimately...)
- Opens in a room, a moment, or a body
- Paragraphs 1 to 3 sentences, rhythm uneven
- Numbers as digits, contractions
- Not a list of exactly 3
- If criticizing a group, you're inside it
- Ends on an image or a blunt binary

## Two tracks

### Track A · Original
Book 1, memoir, proposal, query letter. Saturday 8 to 11:45 am, Sunday 8 to 9:30 am.

1. **MAP** You: outline from your own files
2. **WRITE** You: new pages, forward only
3. **REVISE** You: rework, expand, add or cut
4. **EDIT** You: line edits. Claude: notes only
5. **PROOF** Claude: APA + typo flags. You: yes or no

### Track B · Assisted
Blog, Substack, podcast scripts, captions. Sunday 6 to 7:35 pm, Claude midweek.

1. **SEED** You: idea note, rant, paragraph
2. **EXPAND** Claude: draft with [SEAN] markers (the only step where Claude writes)
3. **EDIT** You: fill markers, cut what isn't you
4. **VOICE** You: read aloud, run the rules
5. **SHIP** Blog repo: Tuesday 6am + Substack

**One-way firewall.** Track B may quote and expand Track A. Track A never takes a sentence from Track B.

## Guardrails

- **Clinical material:** composites only, details changed, nothing traceable to a patient.
- **Your employer:** check UCSD Health's outside-activity and media policy before the book or podcast goes public.
- **Disclosure:** "Every word is mine; I used AI for research and formatting." Track A is built so that's true.
